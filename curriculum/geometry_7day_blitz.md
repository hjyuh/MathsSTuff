## FORMULAS TO MEMORIZE (tape to your wall)

### Coordinate Geometry
- Distance: d = √((x₂−x₁)² + (y₂−y₁)²)
- Midpoint: M = ((x₁+x₂)/2, (y₁+y₂)/2)
- Slope: m = (y₂−y₁)/(x₂−x₁)
- Parallel lines: same slope
- Perpendicular lines: slopes multiply to −1

### Triangle Congruence: SSS, SAS, ASA, AAS, HL

### Similarity: AA, SSS (proportional), SAS (proportional)

### Trig
- sin = opposite/hypotenuse
- cos = adjacent/hypotenuse
- tan = opposite/adjacent
- 30-60-90: sides 1, √3, 2
- 45-45-90: sides 1, 1, √2
- Law of Sines: a/sinA = b/sinB = c/sinC
- Law of Cosines: c² = a² + b² − 2ab·cosC
- Area = ½ab·sinC

### Circles
- Inscribed angle = ½(intercepted arc)
- Central angle = intercepted arc
- Tangent ⊥ radius
- (x−h)² + (y−k)² = r²
- Arc length = (θ/360)·2πr
- Sector area = (θ/360)·πr²

### Area
- Triangle: ½bh
- Parallelogram: bh
- Trapezoid: ½(b₁+b₂)h
- Regular polygon: ½ap (a=apothem, p=perimeter)
- Circle: πr²

### Volume
- Prism/cylinder: Bh
- Pyramid/cone: ⅓Bh
- Sphere: ⁴⁄₃πr³
