## UNIT 1: COORDINATE GEOMETRY (5 problems)

**1.** Find the distance between (−3, 4) and (5, −2). Give exact form.

**2.** Find the midpoint of the segment with endpoints (−7, 2) and (3, −8).

**3.** What is the slope of the line through (4, −1) and (−2, 7)?

**4.** Line m has slope ⅔. What is the slope of a line perpendicular to m?

**5.** Write the equation of the line through (2, 5) parallel to the line y = −3x + 1. Put it in slope-intercept form.

## UNIT 2: TRANSFORMATIONS (5 problems)

**6.** Triangle ABC has vertices A(2,3), B(5,1), C(4,6). What are the coordinates of A′ after reflection over the x-axis?

**7.** Point P(−4, 7) is reflected over the y-axis. What are P′'s coordinates?

**8.** Point Q(3, −5) is rotated 90° counterclockwise about the origin. What are Q′'s coordinates?

**9.** Point R(2, 8) is rotated 180° about the origin. What are R′'s coordinates?

**10.** Point S(−1, 4) is translated by the rule (x,y) → (x+5, y−3). What are S′'s coordinates?

## UNIT 3: TRIANGLE CONGRUENCE & PROOFS (5 problems)

**11.** In triangles ABC and DEF: AB = DE, BC = EF, AC = DF. Which congruence criterion proves △ABC ≅ △DEF?

**12.** In triangles JKL and MNP: ∠J = ∠M, ∠K = ∠N, JK = MN. Which criterion proves △JKL ≅ △MNP?

**13.** In triangles XYZ and PQR, both are right triangles with the right angle at Y and Q respectively. XZ = PR (hypotenuses) and XY = PQ (one leg). Which criterion proves congruence?

**14.** In an isosceles triangle with AB ≅ AC, if ∠B = 70°, what is ∠A?

**15.** A triangle has exterior angle 130° at one vertex. The two remote interior angles are equal. What does each measure?

## UNIT 4: QUADRILATERALS (5 problems)

**16.** In parallelogram ABCD, ∠A = 75°. What is ∠C?

**17.** In parallelogram ABCD, ∠A = 75°. What is ∠B?

**18.** Which quadrilateral has diagonals that are perpendicular AND bisect each other AND are equal length?

**19.** The diagonals of a rhombus are 6 and 8. What is the side length of the rhombus?

**20.** A trapezoid has parallel sides of length 8 and 14, and height 5. What is its area?

## UNIT 5: SIMILARITY (5 problems)

**21.** Triangles ABC and DEF are similar with scale factor 3:2 (ABC to DEF). If AB = 12, what is DE?

**22.** In triangle ABC, line DE is drawn parallel to BC with D on AB and E on AC. If AD = 6, DB = 4, AE = 9, find EC.

**23.** Two triangles are similar. The ratio of their corresponding sides is 2:5. What is the ratio of their areas?

**24.** In a right triangle, the altitude from the right angle to the hypotenuse divides the hypotenuse into segments of 4 and 9. What is the length of the altitude?

**25.** Triangles ABC and DEF: ∠A = ∠D = 60°, AB = 8, AC = 12, DE = 4, DF = 6. Are they similar? Which criterion?

## UNIT 6: RIGHT TRIANGLE TRIG (5 problems, calculator OK for #29-30)

**26.** In a right triangle, the side opposite to angle θ is 5 and the hypotenuse is 13. What is sin θ? cos θ? tan θ?

**27.** In a 30-60-90 triangle, the side opposite 30° is 4. What is the hypotenuse? The side opposite 60°?

**28.** In a 45-45-90 triangle, each leg is 7. What is the hypotenuse (exact form)?

**29.** In right triangle ABC with right angle at C, AC = 8 and BC = 6. Find angle A (to nearest degree).

**30.** In triangle ABC, a = 10, b = 15, angle C = 60°. Find c using Law of Cosines. (Exact form or decimal, your choice.)

## UNIT 7: AREA & VOLUME (5 problems, calculator OK)

**31.** Find the area of a trapezoid with parallel sides 7 and 11, height 6.

**32.** A regular hexagon has apothem 4√3 and side length 8. What is its area?

**33.** A cylinder has radius 5 and height 12. What is its volume? (Leave in terms of π.)

**34.** A sphere has radius 3. What is its surface area? (In terms of π.)

## UNIT 8: CIRCLES (5 problems)

**36.** A central angle of a circle intercepts an arc of 80°. What is the measure of an inscribed angle intercepting the same arc?

**37.** A tangent line touches a circle at point T. The radius to T is 6. How many degrees is the angle between the tangent line and the radius?

**38.** Two chords intersect inside a circle. One chord is divided into parts of length 4 and 9. The other chord is divided into parts of length 6 and x. What is x?

**39.** A circle has equation (x−3)² + (y+2)² = 49. What is the center? Radius?

**40.** An inscribed angle intercepts an arc of 140°. What is the measure of the inscribed angle?

## ANSWER KEY

**Unit 1 (Coordinate Geometry)**
1. √((5−(−3))² + (−2−4)²) = √(64+36) = √100 = **10**
2. ((−7+3)/2, (2+(−8))/2) = **(−2, −3)**
3. (7−(−1))/(−2−4) = 8/−6 = **−4/3**
4. Perpendicular slope = negative reciprocal of ⅔ = **−3/2**
5. Parallel means same slope (−3). Point (2,5): y−5 = −3(x−2), so **y = −3x + 11**

**Unit 2 (Transformations)**
6. Reflection over x-axis: (x,y) → (x,−y), so A(2,3) → **A′(2, −3)**
7. Reflection over y-axis: (x,y) → (−x,y), so **P′(4, 7)**
8. Rotation 90° CCW: (x,y) → (−y,x), so Q(3,−5) → **Q′(5, 3)**
9. Rotation 180°: (x,y) → (−x,−y), so **R′(−2, −8)**
10. **S′(4, 1)**

**Unit 3 (Triangle Congruence)**
11. **SSS**
12. **ASA** (angle-side-angle, since JK is between ∠J and ∠K)
13. **HL** (Hypotenuse-Leg, right triangles)
14. Base angles of isosceles are equal: ∠B = ∠C = 70°. ∠A = 180 − 140 = **40°**
15. Exterior angle = sum of remote interior: 130 = 2x, so each remote interior = **65°**

**Unit 4 (Quadrilaterals)**
16. Opposite angles of parallelogram are equal: ∠C = **75°**
17. Consecutive angles supplementary: ∠B = 180 − 75 = **105°**
18. **Square** (all three: perpendicular + bisect + equal)
19. Diagonals of rhombus bisect at right angles. Half-diagonals are 3 and 4. Side = √(3²+4²) = **5**
20. Area = ½(b₁+b₂)h = ½(8+14)(5) = ½(22)(5) = **55**

**Unit 5 (Similarity)**
21. Ratio ABC:DEF = 3:2, so DE = 12 · (2/3) = **8**
22. AD/DB = AE/EC, so 6/4 = 9/EC, giving EC = **6**
23. Ratio of areas = (ratio of sides)² = (2/5)² = **4/25**
24. Altitude = geometric mean of segments = √(4·9) = **6**
25. Check sides: AB/DE = 8/4 = 2, AC/DF = 12/6 = 2, and ∠A = ∠D is included. **Yes, similar by SAS**

**Unit 6 (Right Triangle Trig)**
26. sin θ = 5/13, cos θ = 12/13 (leg = √(169−25) = 12), tan θ = **5/12**
27. Hypotenuse = 8, side opposite 60° = **4√3**
28. Hypotenuse = **7√2**
29. tan A = opposite/adjacent = BC/AC = 6/8 = 0.75. A = tan⁻¹(0.75) ≈ **37°**
30. c² = 100 + 225 − 2(10)(15)(0.5) = 325 − 150 = 175. c = **√175 ≈ 13.23** (or **5√7**)

**Unit 7 (Area & Volume)**
31. A = ½(7+11)(6) = **54**
32. A = ½ · apothem · perimeter = ½ · 4√3 · 48 = **96√3** (≈ 166.3)
33. V = πr²h = π(25)(12) = **300π**
34. SA = 4πr² = 4π(9) = **36π**
35. V = ⅓πr²h = ⅓π(36)(8) = **96π**

**Unit 8 (Circles)**
36. Inscribed angle = ½ intercepted arc = ½(80) = **40°**
37. Tangent ⊥ radius: **90°**
38. Chord-chord: 4·9 = 6·x, so x = **6**
39. Center **(3, −2)**, radius **7**
40. Inscribed angle = ½(140) = **70°**
