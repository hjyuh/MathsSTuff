## Service

Mahmoud will provide one-on-one tutoring to prepare the Student for the district-issued Honors Geometry acceleration placement test. The goal is for the Student to score 80% or above on the test, qualifying for placement into Algebra 2 the following school year.

## Sessions

- All sessions are conducted via Discord.
- Session length is flexible — sessions run as long as needed.
- Scheduling is flexible and agreed upon between Tutor and Student.
- Sessions continue until the full test curriculum has been covered. There is no cap on the total number of sessions.
- Practice will be assigned between sessions. The Student is expected to complete all assigned practice.
- The Student may ask questions between sessions at any time.

## Payment

- The first session is free. This is a trial session for the Student to determine whether the tutoring is worth continuing.
- A flat fee of **$100** is due in cash after the first session and before the second session begins.
- This fee covers all sessions from enrollment through the test date.
- The fee is the same regardless of how many sessions are needed.

## Refund Policy

### Full refund, no questions asked, if:

- The Student takes the acceleration test and scores below 80%.
- The school cancels the test or changes the acceleration program.

### No refund if:

- The Student quits. Quitting is defined as missing **2 consecutive scheduled sessions** without rescheduling.
- The Student fails to complete assigned practice between sessions.
- The Student uses AI tools (ChatGPT, etc.) to complete assigned practice instead of doing the work themselves.
- The Student does not take the acceleration test.
