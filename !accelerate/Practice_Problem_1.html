Practice Problem — Angle Bisector in an Isosceles Triangle

The setup

Given: △PQR is isosceles with PQ ≅ PR. Point S lies on segment QR, and PS bisects ∠QPR.

Prove: △PQS ≅ △PRS

The figure

PQ ≅ PR (red tick marks). PS bisects ∠QPR (purple arcs at P). PS is shared by both smaller triangles.
tick marks = PQ ≅ PR (given) purple arcs = bisected angle at P green = shared side PS

Before writing the proof, answer these three questions

Q1. The two triangles you're proving congruent are △PQS and △PRS. What piece do both triangles share?
Q2. What does "PS bisects ∠QPR" give you about the two angles at vertex P?
Q3. Count: how many pieces of △PQS ≅ △PRS do you now know to be congruent, and what is the name of the theorem that lets you conclude the triangles are congruent?
