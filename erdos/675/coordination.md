# EP675 Sprint Coordination

## Agent Lanes

1. **Comment/PDF audit**: read the forum proof note and identify exact
   hypotheses, proof steps, and possible gaps.
2. **Squarefree formalization**: prove the general implication from least
   squarefree AP bounds to lower bounds on `t_n`.
3. **Nunes/literature audit**: verify the best current exponent for least
   squarefree numbers in reduced residue classes and whether improvements
   exist.
4. **Translation existence**: reconstruct the sieve proof that squarefree
   numbers have the translation property.
5. **Sums of two squares**: analyze whether forced local congruence conditions
   can produce lower bounds or obstructions.
6. **Prime partition question**: model dense prime partitions and the set of
   integers divisible only by `P` primes.
7. **General forbidden-moduli framework**: formulate necessary/sufficient
   conditions for avoidance sets to have translation property and shift-growth
   lower bounds.
8. **Meta coordinator**: synthesize into a first publishable partial-result
   note and identify the highest-leverage next theorem.

## Non-goals

- No large computation.
- No broad random search.
- No posting until the squarefree proof has been independently checked against
  the exact Nunes theorem.
