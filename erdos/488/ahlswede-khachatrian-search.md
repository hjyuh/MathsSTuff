## THREE RELEVANT PAPERS FOUND

### 1. "Number-theoretic correlation inequalities for Dirichlet densities"
Ahlswede & Khachatrian, J. Number Theory 63, no. 1, 34-46 (1997)

MAIN THEOREM: D̲(A,B) · D̲[A,B] ≥ D̲(A) · D̲(B)

where:
- D̲ = lower Dirichlet density
- (A,B) = {gcd(a,b) : a∈A, b∈B}
- [A,B] = {lcm(a,b) : a∈A, b∈B}

This is an FKG-type inequality on the divisibility lattice.
5.4 suggested applying this to the lcm-lattice coefficients μ_A(d).

### 2. "Density inequalities for sets of multiples"
Ahlswede & Khachatrian, J. Number Theory 55, no. 2, 170-180 (1995)

Title is literally EP-488's domain. Need to access content.

### 3. "On the density of primitive sets"
Ahlswede, Khachatrian & Sárközy (1999)

Studies f-density of primitive sets under various weightings.
Theorem 3: If A is primitive with elements in (N/Q, N], then
Σ_{a∈A} 1/a < c · log Q / (log log Q)^{1/2}

This bounds the harmonic mass of compact primitive clusters —
directly relevant to the compact block analysis.

## CONNECTION TO EP-488

5.4's ancestor matching proof sketch:
"Match each bad signature to the specific earlier layers that created it
and show they pay for it."

The Ahlswede-Khachatrian correlation inequality might formalize this:
if B_j = {b₁, b₂} (two obstructions), there exist "parent" layers with
B = {b₁} and B = {b₂}. The correlation inequality would constrain
the joint density behavior of the child and parent layers.

The 1995 "density inequalities for sets of multiples" might contain
EXACTLY the kind of oscillation bound for sets of multiples that
we need for EP-488.
