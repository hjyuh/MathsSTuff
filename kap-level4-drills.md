## 8.NS — Irrational Number Estimation

**1.** Without a calculator, estimate √(50) + √(18) to the nearest tenth. Show your reasoning.

**2.** Place √(7), √(11), and π in order from least to greatest. Explain how you determined each approximation.

**3.** A square garden has area 72 ft². You need to buy fencing for one side. Fencing is sold in whole feet only. How many feet do you need to buy, and how much fencing is wasted? Express the waste as a decimal.

## 8.EE.A — Scientific Notation & Radicals

**4.** The mass of Earth is approximately 5.97 × 10²⁴ kg. The mass of the Moon is approximately 7.35 × 10²² kg. How many times more massive is Earth than the Moon? Express your answer in standard notation, rounded to the nearest whole number.

**5.** A red blood cell has diameter approximately 7 × 10⁻⁶ meters. A human hair has diameter approximately 7 × 10⁻⁵ meters. A student says the hair is "about 7 times wider." Is the student correct? Explain.

**6.** The distance from Earth to the Sun is about 1.5 × 10⁸ km. Light travels at about 3 × 10⁵ km/s. Estimate the time in minutes for light to reach Earth. Show your work using scientific notation throughout.

## 8.EE.B — Slope & Similar Triangles

**7.** On line ℓ, two students each pick different pairs of points to calculate slope. Student A picks (1, 3) and (4, 9). Student B picks (2, 5) and (5, 11). Using the concept of similar triangles, explain why both students must get the same slope.

**8.** A proportional relationship passes through (0, 0) and (3, 7). The line y = (7/3)x + 2 is graphed on the same axes. Describe the geometric relationship between these two lines and use it to find two additional points on the second line.

## 8.EE.C — Equations & Inequalities

**9.** Write a linear inequality with the variable on both sides that has the solution set x > 5. Then write a DIFFERENT inequality (variable on both sides) with the same solution set.

**10.** For what value of k does the equation 3(2x + k) = 6x + 12 have infinitely many solutions? For what values of k does it have no solution? Explain.

**11.** Solve for x and graph the solution: 4(x - 1) + 3 > 2x + 7 - x

## 8.F.A — Functions

**12.** Give three examples of functions that are NOT linear: one as an equation, one as a table, and one as a verbal description. For each, explain what feature makes it nonlinear.

**13.** Function A: y = 3x + 5. Function B passes through (0, 8) and (2, 12). Which function has the greater rate of change? Which has the greater value at x = 10? Show work.

## 8.F.B — Modeling with Functions

**14.** A pool is being drained. At t = 0 hours it holds 1,200 gallons. At t = 3 hours it holds 750 gallons.
- Find the rate of change and interpret what it means.
- Find the initial value and interpret it.
- Write the function and predict when the pool will be empty.
- Sketch the graph and describe its qualitative features as t increases beyond emptying.

**15.** A graph shows a function that increases quickly, then levels off, then drops sharply. Describe a real-world scenario that could produce this shape. Label the axes.

## 8.G.A — Angles

**16.** Two parallel lines are cut by a transversal. One of the angles formed is 62°. Find ALL eight angles and explain the relationship (alternate interior, corresponding, etc.) that lets you determine each one.

**17.** Can a triangle have angles measuring 45°, 65°, and 80°? Can a triangle have sides 3, 4, and 8? For each, explain the rule that determines whether it's possible.

## 8.G.B — Pythagorean Theorem (3D)

**18.** A rectangular box has dimensions 3 × 4 × 12. Find the length of the space diagonal (corner to opposite corner) through the interior of the box.

**19.** A fly sits at one corner of a room that is 12 ft long, 8 ft wide, and 9 ft tall. What is the straight-line distance from the fly to the opposite corner of the room?

**20.** Point A is at (1, 2, 3) and point B is at (5, 6, 7) in 3D space. Find the distance AB.

## 8.G.C — Volume, Surface Area, Arc Length, Sectors

**21.** A cone has radius 5 cm and slant height 13 cm. Find its volume AND its lateral surface area. (You'll need to find the height first.)

**22.** A sphere has volume 288π cm³. Find its radius and surface area.

**23.** A pizza has diameter 16 inches. You eat a slice with a central angle of 40°. Find the arc length of the crust and the area of the slice.

**24.** A composite solid is formed by placing a hemisphere (r = 4) on top of a cylinder (r = 4, h = 10). Find the total volume and total surface area of the composite figure.

## 8.SP — Scatter Plots & Prediction

**25.** A scatter plot shows hours studied (x) vs. test score (y). The trend line is y = 8.5x + 52. A student studied 7 hours. Predict their score. Another student scored 95 — estimate how many hours they studied. Are both predictions equally reliable? Explain.

---

## Key Formulas (know cold)

- Cone volume: V = (1/3)πr²h
- Cone lateral SA: πr × slant height
- Sphere volume: V = (4/3)πr³
- Sphere SA: 4πr²
- Pyramid volume: V = (1/3)Bh
- Arc length: (θ/360) × 2πr
- Sector area: (θ/360) × πr²
- 3D distance: d = √(Δx² + Δy² + Δz²)
