KasmVNC Browser Workspace MVP

Launch a disposable Chromium workspace from your site.

Each session is a Dockerized Kasm Chromium workspace streamed through your app. The worker stays bound to localhost, and access is bound to your authenticated app session plus a server-issued HTTP-only browser binding cookie.

Use

  1. Sign in with a configured account.
  2. Start a session.
  3. Wait for the embedded KasmVNC workspace to appear.
  4. Use Chromium inside that workspace to open ChatGPT, Claude, YouTube, or another site and sign in there.

Limits

  • Sessions are disposable and terminate when you stop them or when TTL expires.
  • Site credentials stay inside the remote browser session; this app does not rewrite login pages.
  • Production use still needs real user auth, rate limits, and stronger isolation.
